The Juniors Will Master Subjects In Months It Took Us Years To Do

25 November 2026

I am at the end of my career and my memory is not what it once was. In recent years that did not help me at interviews. However, once I returned to a technology I recalled it quickly, after some days, weeks or a little longer.

With AI assistants I have found that not only is recall now more or less instant, I can also learn and absorb new concepts at unthinkable speeds. All that is needed is to put the hours in with the AI tools and sometimes the documentation they reference, e.g. Microsoft Learn, Anthropic Academy etc. That is what I am doing today and it is enjoyable.

If I can learn like this at my age, I am 100% sure the young brains of the future will become knowledge masters in a fraction of the time it took us seniors. Their brains will be wired to learn in a new way that is fully aligned with the AI era. Of course, experience will still need to be gained, but even then risk management and analysis will be at their fingertips.
