It All Feels Too Easy And Strangely Like A Honeymoon

25 November 2026

With the use of AI assistants everything feels too easy and strangely wrong. This is probably because we are in a transition phase where resource constraints and delivery expectations have not yet fully aligned to the AI era, but they will.

After decades of writing code, painful debugging and documentation, it has all become very easy. However, I am reading that the grind is already coming back with the new emphasis on verification and code generation by specification! For those of you who are evangelists, I suggest reading up on Waterfall and the problems of testing too early.
