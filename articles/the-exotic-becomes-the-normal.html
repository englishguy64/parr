The Exotic Becomes The Normal

24 November 2026

Just now the IT development team has shifted to verification mania. I only have one issue with verification, which is the same issue as with testing: you can end up verifying or testing things to death that will never be put into production. I am a great believer in late testing and that won't change with AI.

Once the redundancies in our industry slow down, and the world economy and token costs stabilise, the status quo will return and the exotic will become the normal. In the new age more will be expected from each person. The use of Claude, Codex etc. will be no more exotic than Microsoft Office was in the 1990s and the internet in the early 2000s. The usual competition pressures will return of 'deliver faster' with 'fewer resources' and all that comes with that.

Companies gloating over the flatter management and delivery structures that AI utopians are promising have forgotten the simple mantra 'there is a price for everything'. I leave you to research those for yourself.
